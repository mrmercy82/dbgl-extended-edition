DBGL Extended Edition - New features since DBGL 0.100 beta 17
=============================================================

DBGL was developed up to version 0.100 beta 17 by Ronald Blankendaal
(https://dbgl.org; announcement of 0.100 beta 17:
https://www.vogons.org/viewtopic.php?p=1380082#p1380082). The Extended Edition
is an independent continuation he is not involved in. It mainly adds support
for current DOSBox versions; the foundation and almost everything else are his
work: nearly three quarters of the program code are unchanged from his 0.100
beta 17 (of his roughly 42,000 lines, about 300 were changed or removed;
about 17,000 were added).

Profiles, database and settings are kept when switching.

Extended Edition (source code, downloads, reporting problems):
https://github.com/mrmercy82/dbgl-extended-edition


NEWEST IN 0.101 ALPHA 45
------------------------
  - Get covers for many profiles at once, from all online sources, with a
    preview to tick; ScreenScraper is enabled.
  - One-click update of the Extended Edition (download, checksum, backup,
    restart; usable from alpha 46 on).
  - "Check profiles" recommends DOSBox-X where needed and switches with one
    button.
  - Statistics also as HTML with bar charts or as CSV.
  - Random game by choice (never started, favorites, most or least played);
    play time and sessions for every profile; last DOSBox log in the
    context menu.
  - Long links (up to 2048 characters) are saved (database 0.103, still
    readable by DBGL 0.100 beta 17).


NEWEST IN 0.101 ALPHA 44
------------------------
  - Import GOG and Steam games, recommending DOSBox-X where needed.
  - Find and merge duplicate profiles.
  - Statistics with play sessions, play time by year and month, counts by
    version, genre, developer and year of release; can be saved as a
    report.
  - Toolbar with filter, online info, check profiles, random game,
    statistics and settings.
  - DOSBox output as a log per profile; warning for PC-98 images without
    DOSBox-X; progress bars instead of hangs.


NEWEST IN 0.101 ALPHA 42 AND 43
-------------------------------
  - Replace values in many profiles at once, with preview and a choice of
    the possible values.
  - Find manuals, walkthroughs, maps, reference cards, copy protection and
    soundtracks in the game folders and link them, with icons in the
    context menu "View".
  - Game information from IGDB, SteamGridDB and MobyGames (official API).
  - Optionally all DOSBox settings in the profile, like D-Fend Reloaded.
  - Shader presets of Staging 0.83, "Refresh" and "Try" for shaders.
  - Several CDs: CD2, CD3 ... offered along; CD images mounted the right way
    for each DOSBox version.
  - View buttons List / Tiles / Boxes and a "View" menu; faster start.


MAJOR NEW FEATURES
------------------

1. Full support for DOSBox Staging 0.83
   Renamed settings (e.g. glshader -> shader) and on/off switches are
   converted automatically. Every setting has its own field: CRT colour
   profile, deinterlacing, dedithering, image adjustments, Sound Canvas,
   denoiser, drive noises and more.

2. DOSBox-X up to 2026.10.01
   About 100 common settings have real fields ("Additional options"). The
   configuration is generated completely; in 0.100 beta 17 about half of the
   settings were missing.

3. Every setting of every DOSBox version is reachable
   Settings without a field of their own sit on the matching page in a
   collapsible "Further settings" group: with description, list of values,
   bold for changed values and a reset button.

4. The profile dialog adapts to the DOSBox version
   Option lists come from the configuration of the installed DOSBox. Empty
   pages disappear. The search finds descriptions and values too.

5. Download DOSBox directly
   DOSBox -> "Download DOSBox...": get Staging or DOSBox-X from GitHub (with
   checksum, cancellable, choice of build), unpack it into a folder of your
   choice, add it as a DOSBox version and switch profiles.


MORE NEW FEATURES
-----------------

Keeping profiles in shape
  - Check profiles: find and repair invalid values, missing folders and files,
    fix moved paths for all profiles at once.
  - Warning sign in the profile list with tooltip; a click jumps to the
    setting. "With problems" tab, context menu "Fix problems...".
  - Earlier versions: the last five configurations of every profile.
  - Compare profiles, statistics (play time, most played games).

Many profiles at once
  - Presets (Windows 95/98, Windows 3.1, slow and fast games, Tandy, PC-98,
    your own), also for all selected profiles.
  - Switch to another DOSBox version, with preview, report and a comparison
    of both versions. Whatever would be dropped, including lines added by
    hand to the [autoexec], can be kept line by line.
  - Edit several profiles, including the further settings.
  - Undo the last change of several profiles.
  - Replace values, e.g. output=surface by texture in all selected profiles,
    with a preview and a choice of the possible values.
  - Find manuals, walkthroughs, maps, reference cards, copy protection and
    soundtracks in the game folder and link them in the profile.

Safety and comfort
  - Create and restore backups, optionally automatic.
  - Status window with DOSBox output, errors highlighted, messages clickable.
  - Drop a dosbox.conf on a profile to take over its settings.
  - Lines added by hand to the [autoexec] are no longer lost: kept
    automatically (with or without DBGL's @REM lines) or chosen line by line,
    see Settings -> General.
  - PC-98: browse, start and boot hard disk and floppy images.
  - Remove unused DOSBox versions together with their folder (with a double
    safety question).


SMALLER IMPROVEMENTS
--------------------
  - Play time per game, as a sortable column.
  - German complete, about 7,000 more texts translated in 17 languages.
  - Tooltips stay while the mouse is over them (can be turned off).
  - "Differences from defaults..." for every profile.
  - Check of folders, images and values before starting.
  - Version check at startup, configuration rewritten when needed.
  - Optional notice of new versions of the Extended Edition, pre-releases
    included (Settings -> General, Help -> "Check for updates...").
  - Newer DOSBox versions DBGL does not know yet are mapped correctly.
  - Shader lists with your own shaders, "Sound Blaster 2" page.
  - Help -> "New features..." and "Changelog...".
  - Failed boot: the DOSBox message stays visible.
  - View as list, tiles (with screenshot) or boxes (with cover art): buttons
    at the top right or the "View" menu (Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+7).
  - Game information also from IGDB and SteamGridDB, MobyGames through its
    official API; searching with a Cancel button and understandable errors.
  - Several CDs: CD2, CD3 ... are offered as well; CD images are mounted the
    right way for each DOSBox version (mount or imgmount).
  - Icons for links and the profile configuration in the context menu "View".
  - Shader presets of DOSBox Staging 0.83, refresh shaders, try run.
  - Optionally all DOSBox settings in the profile (as D-Fend Reloaded does).


BUG FIXES COMPARED TO 0.100 BETA 17
-----------------------------------
  - DOSBox-X 2024.10.01 and later: the configuration held only about half of
    the settings.
  - Paths with spaces work (8.3 short names as in DOSBox).
  - Empty entries overrode DOSBox defaults.
  - Boot lines with a Windows path created a broken mount.
  - Assigning another DOSBox version took over old defaults.
  - Reader for older DOSBox configurations fixed.

All changes in detail: Help -> Changelog...
